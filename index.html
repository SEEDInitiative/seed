Who We Are

Bridging the Gap Between Ambition and Opportunity

Founded by students who navigated the complex landscape of international higher education, SEED was created to ensure no Syrian student walks that path alone.

We provide comprehensive mentorship, language support, and educational resources tailored to the unique challenges faced by Syrian youth today.
